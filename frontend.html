Training Program Rules & Policies

Please read through all rules carefully before proceeding to your student dashboard.

📊 Evaluation
  • Technical Topics: 75% (5 topics × 15 marks)
  • CTF: 25% (Completed externally in classroom)
  • Certificate: Minimum 80% overall and 80% in each topic.
  • Scoring below 80% in 3 topics will result in removal from the program after feedback and support are provided.
⚠️ Warnings & Attendance
  • Warnings: 1st: No penalty | 2nd: -10% attendance | 3rd: Removal.
  • Absences: 1st: -5% attendance | 2nd: -10% attendance | 3rd: Removal.
📝 Assessments
  • If you miss an assessment due to a valid excuse, inform the Training Supervisor via WhatsApp before the end of the same day.
  • Approved cases may take the assessment the following day. Only one make-up assessment is allowed during the program.
  • Failure to notify the Training Supervisor before the end of the day will be considered an unexcused absence and a warning will be issued.
📱 Communication
  • WhatsApp with the Training Supervisor is the official communication channel.
  • Always contact the Training Supervisor first, then inform the instructor if needed.
  • Informing the instructor only will not be considered an official notification.
✅ Commitment
  • Students are expected to follow all program rules and maintain professional conduct.
  • Any violation or lack of commitment may result in warnings or disciplinary action.
